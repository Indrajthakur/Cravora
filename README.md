# 🍽️ CRAVORA — Discover Food. Explore Flavours.

**CRAVORA** is a modern and interactive food discovery platform designed to make exploring restaurants and discovering delicious food simple and enjoyable.

Browse restaurants, explore menus, search for dishes, save your favourites, and manage your food cart — all through a clean and responsive interface.

## ✨ Features

- 🏪 **Restaurant Discovery** — Explore different restaurants.
- 🍕 **Food Categories** — Browse dishes by category.
- 🔍 **Search & Filter** — Find restaurants and food items easily.
- 📋 **Restaurant Menus** — Explore available dishes and prices.
- ❤️ **Favourites** — Save your favourite food items.
- 🛒 **Shopping Cart** — Add, remove, and manage items.
- 💳 **Checkout** — Simple checkout experience.
- 📦 **Order Tracking** — View order status and order history.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** — Switch between light and dark themes.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Works across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
